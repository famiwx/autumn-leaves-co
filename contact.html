Archival Reference

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Common notes regarding garment preservation, bespoke fits, and delivery logistics.

We advocate for minimal washing. Virgin wool and heavy-gauge merino possess natural lanolin coatings that repel dust and odors. When required, hand-wash in tepid water with an organic wool rinse, press gently between dry bath towels (never wring), and lay flat away from direct sunlight. Complimentary cedar sachets are supplied with every order to safeguard fibers naturally.

We provide complimentary 30-day domestic returns and size exchanges on all unwashed garments with archival tags intact. Because our collections are crafted in small, numbered editions, we recommend requesting an exchange dispatch immediately to secure your preferred size before inventory is retired.

Yes. We ship worldwide via carbon-neutral tracked couriers. International parcels arrive in custom heavy parchment wrapping with duties calculated at checkout to avoid customs delays. Delivery typically takes 5 to 9 business days depending on global destination.

Every garment page features precise flat garment measurements (chest width, sleeve from spine, center back length). Our outerwear and chunky sweaters are deliberately tailored with an archival ease for layering over thermals. If you prefer a closer, tailored fit, we recommend selecting one size smaller. Our concierge is always happy to offer customized size advice via email or phone.
